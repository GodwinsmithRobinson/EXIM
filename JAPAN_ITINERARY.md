## 1. Trip Overview & Essential Pre-Trip Information

### Why Japan for a First International Trip?

Japan is one of the **safest, cleanest and most family-friendly** destinations in the world — an ideal first international trip. Trains run on the minute, hospitals are world-class, baby facilities exist in almost every public space, and the Japanese people are exceptionally kind and helpful to families with young children.

### Trip Snapshot

| Time Zone | JST — UTC+9 (IST +3:30 hrs) |
| Currency | Japanese Yen (¥ / JPY) |
| Language | Japanese (English widely understood at tourist spots) |
| Emergency Number | 110 (Police), 119 (Ambulance/Fire) |

### Key Japan Facts for First-Time Visitors

- **Safety:** Japan is consistently ranked one of the world's safest countries. Crime rate is extremely low.
- **Cleanliness:** Streets, toilets, trains and restaurants are impeccably clean — perfect for a 7-month baby.
- **Baby-Friendliness:** Multi-functional nursing rooms (授乳室, *jyunyuushitsu*) are available in shopping malls, train stations, department stores and major attractions — always with diaper-changing tables, nursing chairs and a sink.
- **Cash Culture:** Japan is still largely a cash society. Carry ¥10,000–¥20,000 in cash daily. You can withdraw from 7-Eleven and Japan Post ATMs using international Visa/Mastercard.
- **Tap Water:** 100% safe to drink and use for mixing baby formula.
- **Electricity:** 100V, Type A plugs (same as USA). Carry a universal adapter.
- **SIM / Internet:** Buy a tourist data SIM at the airport (IIJmio, Rakuten, or B-Mobile) or rent a pocket Wi-Fi at arrival. Approximately ¥3,000–¥5,000 (~₹1,650–₹2,750) for 5 days.

## 2. Japan Visa & Entry Requirements

### Tourist Visa for Indian Passport Holders

Indian passport holders require a visa to enter Japan. This is a **single-entry tourist visa** processed through the Embassy of Japan in India.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Visa Type | Tourist (Short-Stay — up to 90 days) |
| Processing Time | 5–7 working days (apply at least 4–6 weeks before) |
| Visa Fee | No visa fee for Indian nationals (as of 2024) |
| Where to Apply | Embassy of Japan, New Delhi / Consulates in Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Osaka |
| Application Method | In-person at the embassy/consulate (no postal application) |

### Documents Required for Tourist Visa

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Valid Passport | Valid for at least 6 months beyond travel dates; at least 2 blank pages |
| Visa Application Form | Download from Embassy of Japan website |
| Recent Passport Photo | 45mm × 45mm white background, taken within 3 months |
| Confirmed Round-Trip Flight Tickets | Booking confirmation is sufficient |
| Hotel Booking Confirmation | Confirmed accommodation for all nights |
| Travel Itinerary | Day-by-day plan (this document can serve as the itinerary) |
| Bank Statement | Last 3–6 months showing sufficient funds (₹3–5 lakh+ recommended) |
| Income Tax Returns | Last 2–3 years ITR copies |
| Employer Letter / Business Proof | Leave letter and salary certificate (if employed) or GST/business registration |
| Marriage Certificate | For family applications |
| Child's Birth Certificate | For infant and child |
| Travel Insurance | Minimum USD 30,000 medical coverage |

### Important Visa Notes

- Apply **for all family members separately** — each person needs their own visa application.
- The infant (7 months) also requires a separate visa. Carry the birth certificate.
- **Travel Insurance is mandatory.** Recommended coverage: ₹50 lakh (~USD 60,000) per person.
- Once visa is stamped, you will receive a **sticker visa** in your passport valid for 3 months from the date of issue.

**Budget Tips:**
- Book **3–4 months in advance** for best fares.
- Travel in **shoulder season** (September–November, or late January–February) for lowest airfares.
- **Infant (under 2 years):** Travels free on lap (no seat) on most airlines on international flights. A small infant fare (10–15% of adult fare) may be charged. Confirm with your airline.
- **Request a bassinet seat** (bulkhead row) when booking for the 7-month infant — especially important for a 9+ hour flight. Call the airline directly after booking.
- **Child aged 7:** Full economy ticket required.
- Pack a **baby carrier / soft structured carrier** for the flight and sightseeing.

Tokyo Disneyland is widely considered the **world's best Disneyland** — it is cleaner, more organised and more magical than any other Disney park globally. For your 7-year-old, this will be an unforgettable experience.

> **Important:** Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea are operated by Oriental Land Company (OLC), not Disney US. Tickets must be booked online in advance — walk-up purchase is possible but can lead to sellouts on peak days.

## 10. Family & Baby-Friendly Food Guide

### Eating in Japan with a 7-Year-Old and a 7-Month Baby

Japan is one of the **best countries in the world for food** — and extremely family-friendly. Restaurants here welcome children, and many have dedicated children's menus, high chairs and even toy corners.

### Food Safety for the Baby (7 Months)

| Item | Safe? | Notes |

## 11. Getting Around Tokyo — Transport Guide

### IC Card (Suica / Pasmo) — Your Tokyo Travel Key

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | Pre-loaded rechargeable smart card used on all trains, buses, some taxis and vending machines |
| Where to buy | Narita / Haneda airport; any JR station machine; convenience stores |
| Initial deposit | ¥500 (refunded when returned at departure) |
| Recommended initial load | ¥3,000–¥5,000 per adult |
| Top-up | Any JR or Tokyo Metro ticket machine, 7-Eleven |
| Infant travel | Children under 6 travel **FREE** on all Tokyo trains; no need for separate card |
| Child 7 years | Half-price children's IC card fare applies — purchase child's Suica at station counter |

### Tokyo Metro Day Pass

If you plan heavy metro use (3+ trips per day), consider:

| Pass | Price | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo Metro 24-Hour Pass | ¥600 adult, ¥300 child | Day of heavy metro travel |
| Tokyo Metro 72-Hour Pass | ¥1,500 adult, ¥750 child | 3 days of frequent metro use |
| JR Tokyo Wide Pass | ¥15,000 adult | Day trips to Nikko, Kamakura, Hakone |

### Getting Around with a Pram / Stroller

- All major Tokyo Metro and JR stations have **elevators** (look for blue elevator signs)
- **Priority seating sections** on trains — most Japanese people will happily give up seats
- **Pram-friendly routes:** Use the Ginza, Hibiya or Yamanote lines — wider carriages
- Tokyo Bus system also accommodates prams — use the middle door

### Taxis & Uber

- Taxis in Tokyo are clean, safe and drivers are professional
- **No tipping** in Japan — taxis or restaurants
- Uber works in Tokyo and accepts international credit cards
- For family with infant + luggage: request a **Minivan/Van** option on Uber
